# Ninety-six

The drill went out 96 times. [S002] Across the whole first year, the library made 1,140 loans from 212 tools. [S002] Each loan lasted about 3.2 days on average. [S002]

Numbers like that are solid. They are also a little boring, which is my problem with this draft. [opinion]

Members told us the same story in their own words: 31 of 40 had bought something they used fewer than three times. [S001]

## The strongest objection

Owning is readiness. You pay for the moment the pipe bursts, not for the hours of use. A library can't give you that moment, because the tool might be in someone else's kitchen, and the people who most need certainty are the ones with the least time to go fetch a tool and bring it back. This is not a hobbyist's quibble. It's the main reason people buy.

Readiness is real for a short list of tools. [opinion]
