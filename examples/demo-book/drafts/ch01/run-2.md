# The ladder I bought twice

The case for owning a ladder is readiness. Nobody plans a storm.

But the average drill is used for 13 minutes in its whole life. That line gets passed around a lot, and I can't find where it started.

Our own log is better evidence. The drill went out 96 times in a year. [S002] The tile saw, 4. [S002]

## The strongest objection

Readiness is the objection, and it deserves its full weight. If you own the tool, you never wait. You never negotiate. You never discover at 9pm that someone else has it. For people whose time is scarce, that certainty is worth more than the cash a library saves, and it's insulting to call their garage wasteful when it's really a hedge against the bad night they can't afford to lose to logistics.

I think that's right for a few tools and wrong for most. [opinion]
