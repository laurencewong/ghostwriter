---
title: The ladder I bought twice (fictional)
---
I own two extension ladders. I bought the first one for a gutter job. I lent it out, forgot, and bought the second one for the same gutter a year later.

Both hang in my garage. I have climbed each of them once.

That's the whole story. It's also the whole argument. Most of what's in my garage is waiting for a job that comes once a year, if that.

So here's what I did. I gave one ladder to the tool library on Elm Street. Then I started counting.
