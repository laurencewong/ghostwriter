---
title: The drill is the most popular book in the library (fictional)
---
Our tool library has a drill that goes out almost every other day. Nobody reads it. Everybody borrows it.

I like that. A drill isn't a possession. It's a verb you rent for an afternoon.

What surprised me wasn't the drill. It was the tile saw. Four loans in a year. Four households that didn't buy a tile saw. That's four tile saws that aren't rusting in four garages.

I'm not against owning things. I own a good knife and I'll fight you about it. I'm against owning things you use twice.
