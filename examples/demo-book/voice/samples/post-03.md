---
title: Ten minutes on a bike (fictional)
---
People ask me about the hassle. Isn't it a pain to go get the thing?

Ten minutes there. Ten minutes back. I do it on the way home from the bakery.

Here's my honest answer, though. Sometimes the thing is out. Last month I needed the pipe wrench on a Sunday night and it was sitting in someone else's kitchen. I called a neighbor instead. He had one. We talked for an hour.

I don't know if that's a bug or the whole point.
