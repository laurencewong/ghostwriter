# Thesis options (stage 1 output, fictional demo)

| # | Statement | Arguable | Evidenced | Book-sized | Author-only | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | For most households, borrowing tools from a shared library beats owning them: cheaper, less waste, more neighbors you can call. | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 17 |
| 2 | Ownership makes neighborhoods lonelier. | 5 | 2 | 3 | 3 | 13 |
| 3 | Cities should fund tool libraries like book libraries. | 4 | 2 | 2 | 3 | 11 |

**Option 1. Who disagrees:** people who need tools on their own schedule; a borrowed drill is
useless at 9pm on a Sunday. **Evidence on hand:** S001, S002, S003. **Would prove it wrong:**
members who quit because availability failed them more often than it saved them money.

**Option 2** is the more interesting claim but the evidence is thin (one survey question).
**Option 3** is a policy chapter, not a book.

**Recommendation:** option 1, with option 2 as its most ambitious chapter.
**Question for the author before picking:** are you willing to write the chapter where the
availability problem wins?
