# Stage 3: book spine and argument map

**Read:** `thesis.md`, `sources/index.json`, `interview/` answers.
**Write:** `spine.md`.

1. Break the thesis into 3 to 5 supporting claims. Each claim is a sentence that could be false.
2. Lay out 8 to 12 chapters. Every chapter gets exactly one `thesis_role`:
   - `advances`: makes the case for a supporting claim.
   - `tests`: puts the thesis against its hardest case, and reports honestly what survived.
   - `complicates`: shows where the thesis bends, its limits, its conditions.
   At least one chapter in four must be `tests` or `complicates`. A book that only advances
   is a pamphlet.
3. For each chapter fill the table: claim, role, evidence needed, objection to steelman,
   sources so far.
4. List **Gaps**: evidence needed that no source covers. Each gap becomes an interview
   question (stage 4) or a research task.
