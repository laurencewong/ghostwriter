# Stage 10: revision notes

**Read:** `chapters/chNN.md`, its scoreboard, `thesis.md`, `spine.md`.
**Write:** `revision-notes/chNN.md`.

Sections:
1. **What this chapter now proves**, in one sentence. Compare to its `claim` in the spine.
2. **Open evidence gaps** (`[NEEDS SOURCE]` items, weak citations), each a research task or a
   new interview question.
3. **Thesis pressure:** did anything here push against the thesis? If yes, a Decision /
   Options / Recommendation / What unblocks block for the author.
4. **Spine changes** this chapter implies for later chapters.
5. **Line notes for the author:** at most 10, each with a reason.
